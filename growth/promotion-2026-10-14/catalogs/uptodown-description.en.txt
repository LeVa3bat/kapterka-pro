Kapterka PRO brings quantity-based stock tracking to Android for a small stockroom, warehouse or workshop. Balances by storage location and a movement journal help you check what is available and where an item was transferred.

Add item names and storage locations, record incoming stock or an issue, and transfer items between a stockroom and a work area. A tool returned between locations can be recorded as a reverse transfer. Find a journal entry and compare the recorded balance with the actual quantity.

The main records are stored on the phone. Stock balances and previously entered operations remain available offline. Registration, payment, license verification and cloud synchronization require internet access.

Export XLSX reports including consolidated and location statements, forms No. 8 and No. 18, and a request register. The full trial lasts 72 hours from the first launch. After the trial, Excel export and write-offs using form No. 8 require PRO: currently 490 RUB for 30 days, paid through YooKassa. A license belongs to the app installation ID; a shared inventory code does not transfer PRO access to another phone. When PRO expires, local data is retained and basic operations and viewing remain available.

The APK is free to download. There are no ads. Android 7.0 or later is required. The app interface is in Russian; this English store description is a translation. The current release is 3.8.1 (37).
